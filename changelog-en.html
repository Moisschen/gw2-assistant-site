Changelog

What has changed

When updates arrived and what is new — newest version first.

v0.9.337
26 July 2026
  • Custom markers: create your own categories, add an info text, and paste a screenshot from the clipboard (e.g. of a merchant's menu) — click to view it large right in the game.
  • New marker list with a category filter and an info-text preview per entry.
  • Tekkit's heart-quest descriptions now show up large and readable in the game world instead of tiny.
  • Event reminders now announce the start in good time.
v0.9.330
25 July 2026
  • The map legend can be collapsed and expanded by clicking its title bar.
  • The marker/route and build windows can be closed again via their taskbar icon.
  • The assistant window now closes reliably.
  • Routes (arrows and line) fade out cleanly in sync.
  • Wiki and build views are more robust; links always stay clearly coloured.
v0.9.322
24 July 2026
  • Navigation: show all mastery points worldwide on the map.
  • Reworked legend plus on-foot and mount routes for map completion.
v0.9.319
22 July 2026
  • Detailed wiki info for meta events and world bosses right in the game.
  • Event timer overlay with live countdown; small inline wiki icons.

Older versions: ongoing improvements to navigation, builds, event info and account info.
